Before You Start

Three things to have ready before your first session.

1Install OpenCode

or whatever agent runtime your program admin specifies, on your machine.

2Model provider access

Chiron runs on an LLM - typically a GitHub Copilot license, or an internal Azure AI Foundry endpoint. Confirm with your program admin.

3Sandbox access

A scratch JIRA project and a scratch GitHub repo - never your company's real ones.

Do not point any exercise at a real production JIRA project or GitHub repository. The Dojo is a practice space - mistakes here are free; mistakes in production are not.

How It Works

Two tracks, taken in order, each gated by scenario exercises.

  • Track A - FDE Fundamentals: the role itself - turning an ambiguous business ask into a problem statement, embedding with stakeholders, judging the right delivery mechanism. Nothing here is specific to agentic pipelines.
  • Track B - Agentic SDLC Specialization: how to execute once an agentic pipeline is the chosen delivery mechanism - ticket-state discipline, branch/PR traceability, working alongside build/test agents.

Each unit has a pre-read you work through solo, ending in a self-check question. Bring your answer to Chiron, who will gauge your understanding, go deeper, and decide when you're ready to advance.

Starting Your First Session

Open your agent runtime and say something like:

Chiron, I'm starting the FDE Dojo, Track A Unit 0.

Chiron will gauge what you already know before explaining anything - answer honestly, it shapes how deep the first explanation goes.
