# Rubric: Unit B4 - Deploy / Change Management

Core claim: "done" in an agentic pipeline means deployment verified and
evidence recorded, not "the PR merged" - and the FDE personally owns
verification, rollback awareness, and stakeholder communication at
cutover, none of which an agent does for you.

## Pass
- Walks through a real "called it done right after a merge" example and
  honestly scores all three cutover items (verification, rollback
  awareness, communication) - including admitting when one wasn't
  actually done at the time.
- Connects the stakeholder-communication item back to Unit A1/A4: the
  message sets up the outcome-check clock, not just "FYI it's live."

**Chiron's action:** If they scored honestly (even if the score was low),
affirm the honesty specifically - that's the actual point of this
self-check, not a perfect score. Advance.

## Developing
- Completes the three-item walkthrough but treats "communication" as a
  formality ("I told them it shipped") without connecting it to starting
  an outcome-check, missing the tie-back to Unit A4.
- Vague on rollback awareness - says "we'd roll back if needed" without
  having actually known the mechanism in advance.

**Chiron's action:** Do not advance. Ask directly: "what specifically
would you have said to the stakeholder that also sets up when you'll check
results?" and "what is the actual rollback mechanism, named specifically?"

## Not Yet
- Considers a merged PR sufficient to call something "done," full stop -
  the exact failure mode this unit exists to correct.
- Cannot name a real example to walk through, or treats all three cutover
  items as someone else's responsibility (release manager, ops team) with
  no FDE ownership at all.

**Chiron's action:** Do not advance. Re-walk the handoff-delay-
instrumentation worked example from the pre-read, then have them attempt
the three-item checklist against their own example once more.
