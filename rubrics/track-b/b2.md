# Rubric: Unit B2 - Requirements and Design

Core claim: "agent-ready" means explicit acceptance criteria, scope, and
non-goals - because an agent (unlike a human) cannot ask clarifying
questions when a ticket is vague, it guesses or stalls.

## Pass
- Rewrites a real/plausible vague ask into a ticket with all three
  elements (acceptance criteria, scope, non-goals) explicitly and
  concretely (not restated vaguely, e.g. "scope: the renewals process" is
  too broad - needs a specific named boundary).
- Produces at least two sub-tasks with a correctly identified dependency
  (one genuinely blocks the other, not just "these feel related").

**Chiron's action:** Affirm the specific strength of their acceptance
criteria/non-goals (point to exactly what made it concrete). Ask: "if an
agent read only this ticket with no other context, could it start work
without guessing?" - use their honest answer to decide whether to advance.

## Developing
- Produces a ticket with some elements but one is too vague (commonly:
  non-goals are missing entirely, since they're the easiest to forget).
- Identifies sub-tasks but the dependency direction is backwards or
  unclear (claims A blocks B when actually B's output feeds A).

**Chiron's action:** Do not advance. Point at the specific missing or weak
element (usually non-goals) and have them revise just that part, rather
than rewriting the whole ticket from scratch.

## Not Yet
- Ticket remains at the same vagueness level as the original ask, just
  rephrased ("Improve the renewals process" becomes "Fix the renewals
  process issues").
- Cannot produce any sub-task breakdown, or produces sub-tasks with no
  dependency relationship at all.

**Chiron's action:** Do not advance. Walk through the unit's own
bad/good example side by side with their attempt, asking them to name
specifically what's still missing compared to the "good" example, then
retry.
