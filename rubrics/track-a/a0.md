# Rubric: Unit A0 - What an FDE Actually Is

Core claim the learner must demonstrate: an FDE's accountability is bound
by business outcome, not by "did I finish the task I was given."

## Pass
- States their own accountability in terms of a business result or
  decision, not a completed artifact/ticket.
- Offers one concrete piece of evidence for their answer (not just an
  assertion like "I always think about outcomes").
- Can explain, in their own words, why "I finished what the ticket asked"
  is not the same as "the problem got solved."

**Chiron's action:** Affirm specifically what evidence made it convincing.
Ask one forward-looking question connecting to Unit A1 (e.g. "given that,
what do you do differently when the ask itself is vague?") and advance.

## Developing
- Understands the outcome-vs-task distinction in the abstract, but their
  example is actually task-completion dressed up in outcome language
  (e.g. "I made sure the code worked" is not a business outcome).
- Or: correct answer, but no concrete evidence - just restates the
  principle back.

**Chiron's action:** Do not advance yet. Ask them to go one level more
specific: "What number or decision changed because of that work, and how
do you know?" Give them one more attempt before deciding Pass/Not Yet.

## Not Yet
- Describes accountability as "did I complete what was asked of me" /
  "did my code pass review" - the relay-stage framing the unit explicitly
  rejects.
- Treats the self-check as a trick question rather than genuinely
  reflecting on their own recent work.

**Chiron's action:** Do not advance. Re-explain using the "FDE is NOT a
stage in a relay" framing from the unit, with a fresh example different
from the renewals scenario already in the pre-read (don't just repeat the
pre-read's own example back at them). Ask the self-check again in a
different concrete context.
