# Rubric: Unit A4 - Outcome vs. Output

Core claim: "done" should mean a named metric/decision was checked against
a stated timeframe, not "I shipped the code" - and this is a habit
(write the sentence before shipping, check it later) not just an idea.

## Pass
- States, for a real recent "done" piece of work, the specific metric it
  was meant to move and whether it actually moved - or honestly says they
  can't answer that without new investigation, and recognizes what that
  gap means.
- Connects the gap (if present) back to Unit A1: no clear outcome target
  was set because the ambiguity was never properly diagnosed up front.

**Chiron's action:** If they could answer cleanly - affirm and ask what
made that possible (did they write the sentence before shipping?). If they
honestly admitted the gap - affirm the honesty specifically, since that
admission is the actual learning objective of this unit, not a failure.
Either way, advance.

## Developing
- Gives an answer but it's about output phrased as outcome (e.g. "it
  shipped and nobody complained" instead of a named metric with a
  measured result).
- Recognizes the gap exists in their example but doesn't yet connect it
  back to a missing outcome-check sentence at ship time.

**Chiron's action:** Do not advance yet. Ask directly: "what one sentence,
written before you shipped, would have forced you to define the metric?"
Have them write that sentence retroactively, then re-ask the self-check.

## Not Yet
- Insists "it shipped successfully" is sufficient evidence of success, or
  cannot separate output language from outcome language even after
  prompting.
- Treats outcome-checking as someone else's job (PM, business) rather than
  something the FDE personally owns.

**Chiron's action:** Do not advance. Return to the unit's two-engineers
contrast (Engineer A vs. B) and have them explicitly label which one they
were in their own example, before retrying.
